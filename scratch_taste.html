Mission

Ending AI slop. We want a world of creativity, intentionality, beauty and taste.

That requires cracking how to measure, classify, steer, search over and codify subjective domains into data models can learn from, and tools agents can use.

So that models and agents can produce not just outputs that are correct, but that feel right.

We’re making the unverifiable verifiable, starting with design.
